itisnow Browser

A privacy-focused, highly customizable web browser designed to give users more control over their browsing experience.

itisnow Browser is a modern web browser focused on privacy, security, customization, and user control. It combines Tor support, VPN capabilities, and extensive customization options into one browser.

Features
Built-in Tor

itisnow Browser provides built-in support for browsing through the Tor network.

Connect to the Tor network directly from the browser

Help hide your IP address

Route supported browsing traffic through Tor

Privacy-focused browsing options

Note: Tor can provide strong privacy protections, but no browser can guarantee complete anonymity. Privacy also depends on how the browser and internet are used.

Getting Started

Download and install itisnow Browser, launch the application, and configure your preferred privacy and customization settings.

Depending on the features available in your version, you can choose between standard browsing, VPN-protected browsing, or Tor-based browsing.

Customization

itisnow Browser gives users control over how their browser looks and behaves.

You can customize the interface, appearance, privacy settings, browsing behavior, and other parts of the browser to create an experience that works for you.

Whether you prefer a minimal interface or a highly customized setup, itisnow Browser is designed to let you browse your way.

Privacy and Security

itisnow Browser is designed with privacy and security in mind.

However, Tor and VPN services do not guarantee complete anonymity or security. Browser configuration, extensions, websites, downloads, accounts, and user behavior can all affect your privacy.

For the best security, keep itisnow Browser and all of its dependencies updated.
